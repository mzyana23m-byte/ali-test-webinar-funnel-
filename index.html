FAQ

Questions Attendings Ask First

I already have a full-time job. Do I need to quit before joining?

No. Most physicians inside Velocity run locums alongside their W-2 job while they build the system. You don't need to burn the boat on day one. You need to be ready to start the process now.

Can I use CME funds toward this?

Yes. LocumOS Velocity is an ACCME-accredited educational activity. Many doctors have $3,000 to $5,000 in unused CME funds that can be applied directly toward enrollment, and tuition is typically tax-deductible for 1099 physicians. Confirm your specific number with your institution or accountant.

How much time does this actually take each week?

Two to three hours a week to get you set up in month one. After that, a lot less.

How is this different from working with a locums recruiter?

Recruiters are paid by staffing agencies to fill shifts, not to maximize your rate. Everything here is built to work for you, not the agency.

What if I'm already 1099, or I've done a few locum shifts?

Then the work starts from where you already are: maximizing your rate, optimizing your calendar, and getting you to your goals as quickly as possible.

What happens after month 4?

You keep access to the course for life. Active mentorship concludes at the end of month 4, with an option to stay connected afterward for ongoing job access and the Private Physician Network. No obligation to continue.
